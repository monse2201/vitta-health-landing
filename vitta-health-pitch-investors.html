The Problem

Here's Why They Were Typing.

Instead of focusing on the patient, clinicians are forced to act as data clerks. This necessary documentation splits their attention, compromising the human element of care.

37.5%

Of a clinician's workday is lost to administrative tasks.

This leads to a broken patient experience and is a direct path to physician burnout.

The Solution

Let Doctors Be Doctors.

Vitta Health eliminates manual note-taking. Our ambient AI runs on any device, capturing the patient conversation and automatically generating structured clinical notes. This frees the doctor to be fully present, ensuring the patient feels heard and restoring the human connection in care.

How It Works

From Conversation to Clinical Insight

Our platform transforms natural dialogue into structured, actionable data through a seamless, three-stage process.

1. Capture

Clinicians use any device to effortlessly record patient conversations. No new hardware, no workflow disruption.

2. Analyze

Our AI core transcribes and understands the dialogue, identifying key clinical information with high precision.

3. Structure

The platform automatically generates summarized clinical notes and personalized documents tailored to each medical specialty, ready for immediate review.
