The Problem

Physician burnout is a crisis, fueled by administrative overload.

37.5%

Of a doctor's workday is lost to administrative tasks.

This translates to 2-3 hours per day spent on paperwork instead of patients. This lost time directly impacts revenue potential, quality of care, and drives physician burnout in Latin America.

The Solution

An AI platform that listens, understands, and documents for the doctor.

Vitta Health's ambient AI captures the natural conversation of a clinical visit and automatically generates structured, accurate medical notes **and other clinical documents, personalized for each specialty.**

From SOAP notes for a GP to progress reports for a therapist, our platform adapts to the unique needs of each doctor, giving them back their time.

Transcribing audio...

Generating AI summary...

AI Generated Notes (SOAP)

Subjective: - Patient, Ana Rojas, reports a persistent headache and fatigue for approximately one week. - Notes difficulty concentrating at work. - Denies other associated symptoms. Assessment: - Probable tension headache. - Secondary fatigue. Plan: - Recommend PRN analgesics (Ibuprofen 400mg). - Order blood tests (CBC, TSH) to evaluate fatigue. - Follow-up appointment in 2 weeks to discuss results.
